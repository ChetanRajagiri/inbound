The Technology

Edge AI. No cloud. No lag.

80 frames per second
Every frame of live video is processed locally on an NVIDIA Jetson edge device. No footage leaves the court. No cloud subscription.
Works without internet
All AI inference runs on-device. A bad Wi-Fi day never affects a line call. The system works the moment it's powered on.
Trained on real court data
The model is trained on pickleball-specific footage — not generic object detection. It understands what a bounce looks like on a compact court at speed.
Automatic highlights
The system clips and stores rally highlights automatically. Players can review their best moments — a premium feature courts can offer as an add-on.
