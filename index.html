The Technology

Edge AI. No cloud. No lag.

Edge AI at 80 frames per second
All processing happens locally on an NVIDIA Jetson device. No cloud, no lag, no Wi-Fi dependency. Every line call is made in under 15 milliseconds.
Player tracking and analytics engine
Beyond the ball — the system tracks player positions, rally lengths, and shot placement to build a post-match performance picture for every player.
Automatic highlight generation
The system identifies key moments — winners, long rallies, close calls — and stitches them into a shareable reel automatically. No editing required.
